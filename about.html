Company Story

Creating Memories with Tradition & Design

SriVenkateswara Decoration Centre is dedicated to creating beautiful celebrations with creativity, tradition, and perfection. We specialize in transforming ordinary venues into extraordinary memories through elegant decorations and customized event concepts.

Based in Ethraj Nagar, Ariyalur, Tamil Nadu, we provide our services for all days of the week, helping families and institutions host stunning events. Whether it's a traditional South Indian marriage stage, a lively birthday party backdrop, a school annual day, or a corporate seminar, our team plans and executes setups that perfectly complement the occasion.

Our work values focus on customer-preferred designs, transparent budgets, and strictly delivering settings on time. We take pride in our verified customer rating of 4.0/5, which motivates us to keep raising the standards of event styling in Ariyalur.

Our Mission

To deliver unforgettable celebrations with innovative decoration ideas, professional service, and complete customer satisfaction across Tamil Nadu.

Our Vision

To become one of the most trusted event decoration brands in the region by bringing classic Indian tradition and modern styling elements together.
